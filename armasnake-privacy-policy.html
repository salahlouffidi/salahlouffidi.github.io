Privacy Policy

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salah Eddine Louffidi handles information in the Android game ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION.

1. Information We Collect

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, microphone recordings, or other personal information in order to play the game.

The game may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your Android device, such as round progress, preferences, and game configuration. This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob is a service provided by Google LLC. Depending on Google's advertising configuration and applicable privacy settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as the device IP address, user interactions with the app, diagnostic and performance information, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers. These data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Users can manage or reset their Android advertising ID through Android settings, where supported.

For more information about how Google handles data when using its advertising services, please review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

The application may use the Android vibration feature to provide gameplay and interface feedback. The game does not require access to contacts, SMS, phone calls, camera, microphone, or device location for its core gameplay.

4. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used to operate the game, preserve progress, remember settings, and provide the requested gameplay experience. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is used by Google and its advertising systems for purposes including serving and measuring advertisements, analytics, and fraud prevention, subject to Google's applicable policies and user privacy controls.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent players' personal information. Information collected and processed by Google Mobile Ads may be shared by Google with its advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention systems and relevant service providers or partners as described by Google's policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared by the Android system, the game's data is cleared, the game is uninstalled, or the relevant settings/progress are reset by the game.

Because the game does not require an online player account, we do not maintain a server-side player account or personal profile that requires an account-deletion request. Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices and applicable privacy controls.

7. Children's Privacy

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and age-related treatment are subject to the applicable Google Play, AdMob, and privacy requirements.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the design of the application to avoid unnecessary collection and disclosure of user information. Data collected by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS according to Google's SDK documentation.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
