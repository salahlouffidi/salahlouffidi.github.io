Privacy Policy

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salah Eddine Louffidi handles information in the Android game ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION.

1. Information We Collect

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION does not require an account and, in its current version, does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, microphone recordings, or other personal information in order to play the game.

The game may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your Android device, such as round progress, preferences, and game configuration. This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

2. Device Permissions

The current application may use the Android vibration feature to provide gameplay and interface feedback. The game does not require access to contacts, SMS, phone calls, camera, microphone, or device location for its core gameplay.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used only to operate the game, preserve progress, remember settings, and provide the requested gameplay experience.

4. Sharing of Information

In the current version, we do not intentionally sell, rent, or share personal information collected from players with third parties because the game does not require players to submit personal information to us.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version is designed to operate without requiring an account or a third-party service to play the core game. If third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, or other data-processing services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated as necessary to describe the relevant data practices before that version is distributed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared by the Android system, the game's data is cleared, the game is uninstalled, or the relevant settings/progress are reset by the game.

Because the current game does not require an online account, we do not maintain a server-side player account or personal profile that requires an account-deletion request.

7. Children's Privacy

ARMASNAKE — LOUFFI EDITION does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the current design of the application to avoid unnecessary collection and disclosure of user information. Since gameplay data is primarily stored locally, the security of the device and Android account also contributes to the protection of that local information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
