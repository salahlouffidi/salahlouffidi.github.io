Android backup may include this data according to your device settings. The game does not require an account or directly request your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. When available, use Privacy choices in Settings to reopen the form. Availability depends on your region, app version and the publisher's AdMob configuration. Android also provides advertising preferences in system settings. Local data and deletion Use the available game reset controls or clear the app's storage in Android settings to remove local game data. Android backups may restore it; manage backups separately in system settings. Contact the developer about information you provide directly. Google manages data processed by its services under its own policies and controls. Security and retention Game saves remain until removed or reset, subject to Android backup behavior. Third-party service retention follows provider policies. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Updates This policy may change when features or processing change.
