WalkingSafe: Privacy policy Effective date: 14 November 2026 Developer: Salah Eddine Louffidi (LOUFFI) Contact: salahlouffidi@gmail.com Camera WalkingSafe uses the rear camera only to show a live preview on your own device, as a see-through layer behind other apps. Camera frames are not recorded, saved, analysed or uploaded, and the app does not read content in other apps. Data collection and sharing WalkingSafe does not collect or share personal data. There are no ads, analytics, accounts or developer-operated servers, and the app does not request internet access. Permissions Camera and "Display over other apps" are needed to show the camera layer. Notifications are optional and only add a Stop control. You can stop the layer or revoke these permissions in Android settings at any time. Settings stored on your device Your language choice, transparency level and whether you have watched the introduction video are saved on your device. Android backup or device transfer may include these settings according to your device settings. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove them; manage backups in Android settings. Safety WalkingSafe does not detect obstacles or traffic and cannot guarantee your safety. Stop before reading or typing. Never use the app while driving, cycling or crossing a road. Updates This policy may change when features change. Check the effective date above.
